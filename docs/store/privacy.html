InventoryPro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

InventoryPro is a private inventory, job, and crew management application operated for authorized company personnel. Accounts are provisioned by a company administrator; the app is not intended for general public use.

Information the app collects

  • Account information — your name, role, and login credentials (PINs are stored only as secure hashes).
  • Work records — inventory items, job and vehicle records, service entries, and messages you create in the app.
  • Photos and media — pictures you take or attach (for example receipts and job documentation), using the camera or photo library with your permission.
  • Location — approximate device location, captured only while the app is in use and only with your permission, to give inventory and media records job-site context.
  • Device push token — used solely to deliver work notifications you have enabled.
  • Activity records — an audit log of changes made in the app, kept for accountability within the company.

How information is used and stored

All data is synced to and stored on the company's own private servers. Data is used only to operate the inventory, job, and communication features of the app. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not share data with third parties for advertising or analytics.
  • The app contains no ads and no third-party tracking.

The only third-party service involved in operating the app is Expo's push notification delivery service, which processes device push tokens and notification content in order to deliver notifications.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained while your account is active or as required for company records. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data — or to delete your account — contact your company administrator or email the address below. Account deletion requests are honored by removing the account and its personal data from the active system.

Children

InventoryPro is a workplace tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
